# Scoped Working Session Agenda (Template)

*Vendor and integrator meetings work best as scoped working sessions: a fixed purpose, an agenda sent in advance, and a clear list of what's out of scope. An open-ended "let's hop on a call" tends to turn into live support, and whatever gets answered on that call becomes the expectation for the rest of the project.*

**How to use it:** send the email below at least one business day before the session, with the agenda filled in. If the integrator can't name the questions they want answered, the session probably isn't needed yet. Point them to the documentation first.

## The Invitation Email

> Subject: Working Session Agenda: [Integration Name], [Date]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Looking forward to our session on [date] at [time, time zone]. So we can make the most of the [30/45/60] minutes, here's the plan.
>
> **Goal:** [one sentence, e.g., "Confirm the connector user can reach every object in the approved scope, and agree on the production cutover date."]
>
> **Before the session, please:**
> - Review [link to the relevant documentation page]
> - Send any error messages or logs you'd like us to look at, so we can review them ahead of time
> - Confirm who from your team will attend
>
> **Agenda:**
> 1. [Topic] ([N] min)
> 2. [Topic] ([N] min)
> 3. Decisions and next steps (5 min)
>
> **Out of scope for this session:** [e.g., reviewing connector code, changes to the approved scope, production access]. Scope changes go through the scoping document as a separate request.
>
> Talk soon,
> [Signature]

## The Follow-up Email

Send it the same day, while the details are still fresh.

> Subject: Recap: [Integration Name] Working Session, [Date]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> Thanks for the time today. Here's what we agreed:
>
> **Decisions:**
> - [Decision]
>
> **Next steps:**
> 1. [Action], [owner], by [date]
> 2. [Action], [owner], by [date]
>
> Anything raised today that falls outside the approved scope ([item]) will come through as a change request on the scoping document.
>
> [Signature]

## Why This Format Holds Up

- **The pre-read does half the work.** Questions answered by the documentation get answered before the meeting, and the time goes to what the documentation can't cover.
- **"Out of scope" written down is easier to hold** than "out of scope" said on a call. It's also kinder: nobody gets surprised by a boundary halfway through the meeting.
- **The recap becomes the record.** When someone later recalls the meeting differently, there's a same-day written summary everyone received.
